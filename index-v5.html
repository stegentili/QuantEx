FAQ

Questions, answered.

The key concepts behind Quantex, explained simply.

What does "quantum-safe" actually mean for me?+
Most wallets today are protected by cryptography that a powerful enough quantum computer will eventually be able to break. Quantex uses zkSTARK proofs instead of that older elliptic-curve cryptography, so your funds and trades stay secure even after quantum computers arrive.
What is Q-day, and should I worry about it right now?+
Q-day is the day a quantum computer becomes powerful enough to break the encryption most crypto wallets rely on today. Google has set 2029 as its own deadline to be fully migrated. You don't need to panic today, but any funds secured with old-style cryptography will be exposed once it arrives - Quantex is built to be safe before and after that date.
What's a zkSTARK proof, in plain English?+
It's a way to prove that a trade or transaction is valid without revealing the private details behind it, using math that quantum computers can't break. Think of it as a tamper-proof receipt - anyone can check it's real, but no one can forge it or read what's inside.
Is Quantex its own blockchain?+
Yes. Quantex is a dedicated, quantum-safe Layer-1 blockchain built from the ground up for trading - not an app bolted onto someone else's chain. Every trade you place is matched and settled natively on it, secured end to end by Starkom, our open-source zkSTARK engine.
How does the dark pool keep my large orders private?+
When you route an order through the dark pool, its size, direction, and your wallet address stay hidden behind a zkSTARK proof - only the execution price is ever public. That means you can move size without tipping off the market or getting front-run.
Does Quantex ever hold my funds?+
No. Quantex is non-custodial, so your assets stay in your own wallet at all times. Every trade is matched on our order book and settled on-chain, where anyone can verify it happened correctly.
Do I get charged for cancelling an order?+
No, cancellations are always free. You can place and cancel orders as fast as your strategy needs without paying a fee just to change your mind.
